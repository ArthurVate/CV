My Approach

Product Operating System

The mental models and processes I bring to every product - from discovery through scale.

01

Discover Before Building

Every feature starts with a real customer problem, not a stakeholder request. I run CustDev interviews, mine support data, and map behavioral patterns before writing a spec.

02

Hypothesize → Test → Learn

The roadmap is a portfolio of bets, each with a testable hypothesis and a predefined success metric. A/B tests are the ground truth. Opinion is the starting point, not the ending point.

03

Align Teams on Outcomes

I share context and metrics, not just tasks. Engineers and designers make better decisions when they understand the "why" behind the "what." Alignment scales; micromanagement doesn't.

04

Ship Fast, Iterate Faster

Bias toward getting something real in front of users quickly. Learn from behavior, not assumptions. An imperfect thing shipped beats a perfect thing in the backlog every time.

05

AI as a Force Multiplier

I integrate AI tools at every stage - research, ideation, drafting, creative production, analytics. The goal: multiply team output and capability without growing headcount proportionally.

06

Full-Funnel Ownership

I track every metric from first impression to long-term retention. Acquisition, activation, engagement, revenue, referral - each stage is part of the product and my responsibility.
